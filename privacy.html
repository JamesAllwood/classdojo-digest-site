Privacy policy

ClassDojo Digest is a personal, single-user application run by its owner for their own household. It accesses the owner's Google Calendar to create and update events, and the owner's Gmail account to send a digest email to the owner. It reads ClassDojo content that the owner can already see in their own ClassDojo account.

Data is stored only in the owner's private database and Google account. No data is shared with or sold to third parties, and no other users' data is collected. Google user data is used only to provide these features and is not used for any other purpose.

To have any stored data deleted, revoke the app's access in your Google Account settings and contact the owner.
